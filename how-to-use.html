# How to Use VocalWitness VocalWitness is a public square for sealed citizen reports. Anyone can speak. Once a report is sealed, the media and core record are protected from ordinary editing or hard deletion. --- ### 1. Post a Citizen Report (Public Square) 1. Go to the Public Square on the home page. 2. Write what you saw or experienced (max 3,000 characters). 3. Optionally add a photo, short video, or live voice recording. 4. Choose whether to post as yourself or anonymously (if available). 5. Tap **Publish Report**. What happens next: - Your media is cleaned and hashed on your device. - The report is sealed and added to the public record. - You can edit the **text only** for 30 minutes. - After 30 minutes, you can only soft-hide the report. Media, hashes, and the seal cannot be changed by ordinary users. --- ### 2. Post a higher-integrity Witness Voice report Use **Witness Voice** when you want stronger integrity for first-hand observations. 1. Open the Witness Voice page. 2. Record your voice or write your observation. 3. Optionally attach photo or video. 4. The system hashes the final media on your device before upload. 5. Publish once. The report is sealed. Witness Voice reports carry higher weight in the system and are designed for independent verification. --- ### 3. Download and share an Evidence Pack After publishing: 1. Click **Download Evidence Pack**. 2. You receive a JSON file containing: - Text hash - Media hashes - Pack core hash - Timestamps and verification instructions 3. Share the link or the JSON file with journalists, NGOs, or anyone who needs to verify. Anyone can verify without an account. --- ### 4. How anyone can verify a report **Online (easiest)** - Open the shared link or go to the Verify page. - Paste the report ID + hash, or drop the Evidence Pack JSON. - You can also drop the original media file to check the hash. **Offline** 1. Download the media file and the Evidence Pack. 2. Compute the SHA-256 of the media file on your computer. 3. Compare it with the hash inside the Evidence Pack. 4. Do the same for the text body. --- ### 5. Build your Circle - Follow people whose reports you trust. - Higher trust levels (phone verification and Witness Circle) unlock stronger circle features. - Your circle helps you see reports from voices you choose. --- ### 6. Live Arena - Start or join real-time community rooms. - Useful for ongoing events or group reporting. - Verified citizens have higher limits. --- ### 7. Useful tips - Turn on **Data Saver** if you are on a slow or expensive connection. Images and audio will be compressed more aggressively. - Always speak clearly when recording live voice — it carries stronger evidence weight. - Never post something you did not personally witness if you are using the higher-integrity channels. - Keep your Evidence Packs. They are your permanent verification files. --- ### Need more help? See the [FAQ](/faq) for common questions about sealing, privacy, verification, and trust levels.
