How to Use VocalWitness

A complete guide to speaking out, sealing evidence, and verifying the public record.

VocalWitness is a privacy-first public square for citizen reporting. Anyone can share what they witnessed or join live discussions. Once a report is published, its media and cryptographic seal are protected against secret edits or hard deletion.

1. Understand the Two Channels

VocalWitness separates open community discussion from high-integrity first-hand reporting so readers know what kind of record they are viewing:

🗣️ Citizen Talk (Public Square)

Open to all signed-in citizens for everyday reports, community updates, questions, and civic discussion. Every post is still hashed and sealed.

🛡️ Witness Voice (Higher Integrity)

Reserved for direct, first-hand observations and verifiable evidence. Media is fingerprinted on your device before upload for stronger verification.

2. Publish a Sealed Report

  1. Sign in with Google, GitHub, or X (no government ID or passport is ever collected).
  2. Open the composer in the Public Square and choose Citizen Report or Field Note (if posting in a professional capacity like Researcher, NGO, or Educator).
  3. Write what you saw or experienced clearly, and add an optional short headline.
  4. Attach Live Voice, a Photo, a short raw Video, or an Audio file if you have supporting evidence.
  5. Choose whether to post publicly or restrict visibility to your Trusted Circle, then tap Publish Report.

What happens when you publish:

• Location (GPS) and camera metadata are automatically stripped in your browser.

• A SHA-256 digital fingerprint is created and sealed into the public record.

• You have 30 minutes to fix typos in the text only. Media and hashes can never be altered.

• After 30 minutes, you can soft-hide a report from the main feed, but the sealed record cannot be secretly erased.

3. Record Live Voice & Witness Voice Reports

Use Witness Voice when you are directly witnessing an event and want the strongest level of integrity.

  1. Switch to the Witness tab or select Witness Voice as your destination channel.
  2. Tap Live Voice to record directly on the spot—live audio carries stronger weight as first-hand evidence than uploaded files.
  3. Attach unedited photos or short raw video clips (AI-generated or heavily edited videos are strictly prohibited).
  4. Review your preview and publish once to lock the cryptographic seal.

4. Download & Share Your Evidence Pack

  1. Right after publishing (or from any sealed report), click Download Evidence Pack.
  2. Save the JSON file—it contains your report ID, timestamp, text hash, media hashes, and verification instructions.
  3. Share the report link or Evidence Pack JSON with journalists, human rights groups, researchers, or your community.

No account is required to inspect or verify an Evidence Pack.

5. Verify Any Report or Media File

Online Verification (Easiest)

Open the shared report link or visit the Evidence Verifier page. You can check a Testimony or Credential ID against the ledger, scan a media file for Content Credentials (C2PA), or paste an Evidence Pack JSON to confirm nothing was altered.

Offline Verification

Compute the SHA-256 hash of the original media file or text on any computer and compare it against the hash inside the Evidence Pack. Identical hashes prove the file has not changed by a single bit since sealing.

6. Live Arena, My Circle & Trust Tiers

  • Live Arena: Join or host real-time audio rooms for breaking local events, traffic, weather, or civic updates, filtered by Near Me, My Circle, or Higher Trust.
  • My Circle: Follow reliable reporters and build a Trusted Circle so you can share sensitive updates only with people you trust.
  • Verification Tiers: Everyone starts as a Citizen. Complete a temporary anti-spam phone check (never stored publicly) to unlock Citizen Circle, or complete Higher Trust verification for Witness Circle features.
  • Groups & Corroboration: Join topic or community groups and corroborate reports you independently witnessed to strengthen public context.

7. Safety, Data Saver & Quick Controls

  • Privacy Shield: Keep Privacy Shield enabled in Edit Profile to hide your real name and region from public view.
  • Data Saver Mode: Toggle Data Saver in the top menu or footer to reduce bandwidth and mobile data usage on slow connections.
  • Emergency Clear: In Security & Account settings, use Emergency Clear This Device to instantly wipe local browser data on your phone or computer while keeping your sealed reports safe on the record.
  • Keep Your Evidence Packs: Store downloaded JSON packs in a safe folder or cloud backup as permanent, independent proof.
