How to Use VocalWitness

Simple steps to seal and share what you witnessed.

VocalWitness is a public square for sealed citizen reports. Anyone can speak. Once a report is sealed, the media and core record are protected from ordinary editing or hard deletion.

Client-side SHA-256 30-minute text window Soft-hide only

1. Post a Citizen Report (Public Square)

  1. Go to the Public Square on the home page.
  2. Write what you saw or experienced (max 3,000 characters).
  3. Optionally add a photo, short video, or live voice recording.
  4. Choose whether to post as yourself or anonymously (if available).
  5. Tap Publish Report.
What happens next:
• Media is cleaned and hashed on your device.
• The report is sealed and added to the public record.
• You can edit the text only for 30 minutes.
• After 30 minutes you can only soft-hide the report. Media and seal cannot be changed.

2. Post a higher-integrity Witness Voice report

Use Witness Voice when you want stronger integrity for first-hand observations.

  1. Open the Witness Voice page.
  2. Record your voice or write your observation.
  3. Optionally attach photo or video.
  4. The system hashes the final media on your device before upload.
  5. Publish once. The report is sealed.

3. Download and share an Evidence Pack

  1. After publishing, click Download Evidence Pack.
  2. You receive a JSON file with text hash, media hashes, pack core hash, and verification instructions.
  3. Share the link or the JSON file with journalists, NGOs, or anyone who needs to verify.

Anyone can verify without an account.

4. How anyone can verify a report

Online (easiest)

Open the shared link or go to the Verify page. Paste the report ID + hash, or drop the Evidence Pack JSON. You can also drop the original media file to check the hash.

Offline

Download the media and the Evidence Pack. Compute the SHA-256 of the media file on your computer and compare it with the hash inside the pack. Do the same for the text body.

Useful tips

  • Turn on Data Saver if you are on a slow or expensive connection.
  • Speak clearly when recording live voice — it carries stronger evidence weight.
  • Only submit first-hand observations on higher-integrity channels.
  • Keep your Evidence Packs. They are your permanent verification files.
