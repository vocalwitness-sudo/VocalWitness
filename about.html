About VocalWitness

Your Voice. Your Truth. Shared with Integrity.

Our Mission

In an era of synthetic deepfakes, coordinated misinformation, and centralized narrative control, VocalWitness restores the public square. We believe truth is not dictated by algorithms or political gatekeepers — it is evidenced, witnessed, and notarized by real people.

We empower every citizen to publish raw testimonies, forensic media, and lived experiences using client-side cryptographic hashing and privacy tools — ensuring absolute authenticity while safeguarding user privacy.

Built on enterprise infrastructure: Google Cloud / Firebase Cloudflare Edge Shield W3C Web Crypto & ZK Proofs
🔬

Forensic Integrity

Photos, audio, and documents receive local SHA-256 cryptographic hashing. Zero-Knowledge proofs ($\text{zk-SNARKs}$) allow you to verify authenticity without revealing private identifiers.

🛡️

Privacy & Voluntary Choice

Speaking on record is your choice. Camera EXIF metadata is scrubbed automatically, and temporary phone verification is strictly used to block spam without saving phone numbers on public ledgers.

🌍

Community Stewardship

No single administrator controls the narrative. Trust is earned through honest participation, quadratic voting, and transparent witness reputation scores.

🤝

Community Supported

Sustained through voluntary community grants and non-custodial crypto sponsorships. No paywalls, no tracking ads, and no premium features hidden behind money.

How We Build Trust

1. Citizen Talk → True Witness progression

Begin as a Citizen and earn higher verification tiers through anti-bot verification and good-faith participation. True Witness mode unlocks full cryptographic notarization.

2. Tamper-Proof Evidence Ledger

All published evidence is timestamped and cryptographically cataloged. The Evidence Ledger creates an immutable record that prevents post-publication tampering.

3. Un-manipulated Distribution

We operate purely as a neutral, un-manipulated public square and distribution medium. We do not edit, shadowban, or artificially boost content.
