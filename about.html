About VocalWitness

Your Voice. Your Truth. Protected by Design.

What is VocalWitness?

VocalWitness is a simple public square where anyone can share what they saw or experienced — with proof that it hasn’t been changed.

In a world full of edited videos, fake news and controlled narratives, we give ordinary people a safe place to publish raw testimonies, photos, voice notes and documents. Everything is protected with strong cryptography so the evidence stays trustworthy and your privacy stays intact.

Secured by enterprise-grade technology: Google Cloud / Firebase Cloudflare Edge Shield W3C Web Crypto & Zero-Knowledge Proofs
🔬

Proof That Nothing Was Changed

Every photo, voice note and document is given a unique digital fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) right on your device before it is uploaded. Zero-Knowledge proofs let others confirm the evidence is real without seeing your private details.

🛡️

Your Privacy Comes First

Speaking out is always your choice. We automatically remove camera location and device information. Phone verification is only temporary — used to stop spam bots — and is never stored on any public record.

🌍

Community, Not Control

No single person or company decides what is true. Trust grows through honest participation, transparent reputation scores, and community tools. We stay neutral.

🤝

Free Forever for Everyone

VocalWitness is kept alive by voluntary community support and crypto donations. There are no ads, no paywalls and no premium features locked behind payment.

How Trust Works Here

1. Start Simple, Grow Stronger

Everyone begins as a Citizen. You can raise your verification level with simple anti-bot checks and good behaviour. Higher levels unlock stronger cryptographic protection (True Witness mode).

2. Tamper-Proof Evidence Ledger

Once something is published, it is timestamped and locked into a permanent chain. No one — not even us — can quietly change or delete the original record.

3. We Don’t Control the Narrative

VocalWitness is a neutral public square. We do not edit posts, shadow-ban people, or push certain stories higher. The evidence speaks for itself.

“In the age of noise, the clearest voice is the one that can prove it was never altered.”
