About VocalWitness

Your voice. Your truth. Protected.

What is VocalWitness?

VocalWitness is a privacy-first public square where anyone can share what they saw or experienced — with strong protection so reports cannot be quietly rewritten. We start in Africa — rooted in Nigeria (including Enugu and Lagos) — and grow outward.

In a world full of edited media and noise, ordinary people get a place to publish photos, voice notes, and reports. Media is fingerprinted on your device; sealed entries are designed to stay on the public record.

Protected by: Google Cloud / Firebase Cloudflare Edge Web Crypto • Hash seals Membership proofs (evolving)
🔬

Proof Nothing Was Changed

Photos, voice notes, and files can receive a SHA-256 digital fingerprint on your device before upload. Others can check that a download matches the sealed hash via Evidence Packs and the Verify page.

🛡️

Your Privacy Comes First

Speaking out is your choice. We strip routine location and device metadata from media where privacy mode applies. Phone checks are for anti-spam; they are not written onto the public sealed ledger as your identity.

🌍

Community, Not Control

No single person decides what is true. Trust grows through participation, corroboration, and clear reputation signals. We aim to stay neutral infrastructure — not an editorial channel.

🤝

Free to Report. Funded with Care.

Publishing sealed reports, using privacy tools, reading the Square, and basic verification stay free — no ads on testimony, no paywall on the public record. Voluntary Support the Square contributions and an optional Independent Citizen Press Credential (for professionals who need a formal PDF) help pay for servers and development. Trust tiers are never for sale.

How Trust Works Here

1. Start simple, grow stronger

Everyone can begin in open citizen reporting. Optional phone verification helps reduce spam (Citizen Circle). Higher Trust / Witness Circle is earned through platform checks and membership safeguards — not by payment.

2. Protected public record

Once a report is sealed and published, the original is designed so it cannot be quietly edited or deleted through normal user actions. Integrity rests on client-side hashing, server rules, and auditability tools.

3. We don’t own the narrative

VocalWitness is a public square for evidence-bearing speech. We provide structure and safety rails; we do not rewrite your report to fit a storyline.

How the Square stays online

Infrastructure, storage, and continuous development cost real money. We fund the project without turning citizen reporting into a payment product:

  • Free core — report, read, basic Evidence Pack, Verify page
  • Voluntary support — optional gifts to keep the Square independent
  • Optional press credential — formal PDF for professionals who need it
  • Partners & grants — aligned studios, civic, and journalism support where available

Paying never buys a higher truth rank. Supporter recognition is thank-you only; sealed integrity and trust tiers follow the same rules for everyone.

⚙️ Technical details (journalists, researchers & developers)

Media & evidence integrity

Media can be hashed locally with SHA-256 before upload. Evidence Packs and the Verify flow let third parties recompute hashes and compare them to sealed metadata. Entries may be hash-linked for tamper evidence at the application layer.

Membership & higher trust

Higher Trust / Witness Circle uses a membership commitment and nullifier model so elevation cannot be casually replayed. A Circom/Groth16 membership circuit (Poseidon, Merkle inclusion, trust and activity thresholds) is part of the roadmap and artifact pipeline. Production elevation is being hardened toward full client-side proving and server-side verification; until that path is complete, treat membership badges as platform higher-trust status, and treat media hash seals as the primary cryptographic integrity story for evidence files.

Privacy architecture

Client-side EXIF/GPS scrubbing in privacy mode • Edge protection via Cloudflare • Phone verification aimed at anti-spam, not as a public identity on the sealed ledger • Data minimization in product design.

Trust tiers

• Citizen Talk — open participation
• Citizen Circle — phone verification (anti-spam)
• Witness Circle / Higher Trust — stronger membership checks and evidence tooling
• Reputation grows with honest use; it is not a paid ranking product

Immutability model

Application-level sealing: Firestore security rules (including no ordinary delete of sealed testimonies), client-side hashing, and operational audit logs. This is not a full public blockchain. Optional public-chain or IPFS-style anchoring may come later; it is not required for day-to-day hash verification.

Independent checks

See the journalist verification notes in our docs: download an Evidence Pack, recompute SHA-256 on the media, and compare to sealed metadata. Use Transparency and Verify for ledger-oriented checks as those surfaces mature.
