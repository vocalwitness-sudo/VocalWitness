Safety & Your Rights

Your Right to Speak the Truth

Every person has a natural right to share what they have witnessed. This right is protected worldwide under Article 19 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights — the freedom to hold opinions and to share information without interference, no matter where you live.

Protected by: Google Cloud / Firebase Cloudflare Edge Shield Zero-Knowledge Cryptography

1. Strong Protection by Design

We cannot give away information that we never collected. VocalWitness uses local hashing and zero-knowledge technology so your identity stays protected.

  • No identity logs: We do not store your IP address with your public posts.
  • Automatic cleaning: Camera details and GPS location are removed in your browser before anything is uploaded.
  • Mathematical protection: Even if authorities ask, we have no private keys or hidden data that can unmask anonymous witnesses.

2. Extra Safety Tips for High-Risk Situations

📡

Safer Internet Connection

When you are in a restrictive area, use Tor or a trusted VPN. Avoid publishing on mobile networks that are directly linked to your government-issued SIM card.

📱

Device Safety & Quick Exit

Protect your phone with a strong passcode (not just fingerprint or face unlock). If you feel in danger, press the red Quick Exit button — it immediately clears local data and leaves the site.

3. What Is Not Allowed

To keep the platform safe and credible for everyone, these actions are strictly forbidden:

  • ✕ Doxxing: Sharing someone’s private phone number, home address, or ID details without their clear permission.
  • ✕ Harassment & Threats: Intimidating people, encouraging violence, or making personal attacks.
  • ✕ Fake or Altered Evidence: Uploading deepfakes or edited media meant to falsely accuse or harm others.
  • ✕ Spam & Fake Accounts: Using bots or many fake accounts to manipulate the community.

4. How We Keep the Community Safe

Safety comes from community reporting, automatic checks for harmful language, and clear verification levels:

• Citizen Talk — Open discussion for everyone.

• True Witness — Higher-trust level with cryptographically protected evidence and reputation scores.

Our Goal:

A safe public square where real testimonies can be shared, people are protected by design, and technology helps truth travel freely.
