Safety & Community Guidelines

VocalWitness is a public square built for truth, evidence, and respectful dialogue. We protect good-faith witnesses with world-class infrastructure while keeping our public record resilient against bad actors.

Security architecture backed by: Google Cloud / Firebase Cloudflare Edge Shield Client-Side EXIF Scrubbing

1. Core Principles

🌟

Speak Truthfully & Freely

Share what you genuinely witnessed or experienced. Whether you choose to attach your name or publish under zero-knowledge proofs, honest testimony builds public trust.

🤝

Protect the Public Square

Disagreement and debate are essential. Targeted harassment, malicious doxxing, or manufacturing fake evidence undermines truth for everyone.

2. Prohibited Conduct

To keep VocalWitness safe and credible, the following are strictly forbidden:

  • ✕ Doxxing & Contact Exposure: Publishing private third-party phone numbers, home addresses, or financial IDs without consent.
  • ✕ Targeted Harassment & Threats: Intimidation, incitement to violence, or hate speech against individuals or vulnerable groups.
  • ✕ Evidence Tampering & Synthetic Media: Uploading manipulated deepfakes or fake evidence designed to frame innocent people.
  • ✕ Spam & Bot Networks: Automated spamming, commercial scams, or deploying sybil accounts to manipulate democratic voting.
  • ✕ Exploitative Content: Sexually explicit, non-consensual, or violent exploitation media.

3. OpSec & Personal Safety Tips

💡 Smart Operational Security for Witnesses
  • Your Identity is Your Choice: Stating your name or speaking on record is completely voluntary. If you prefer privacy, VocalWitness automatically scrubs photo camera metadata (EXIF/GPS tags) before saving.
  • Keep Private Details Private: Avoid writing your personal phone number, private home address, or payment credentials inside public testimony text.
  • No Phone Tracking: Phone verification is used strictly as a temporary bot-prevention gate. We do not store or display your phone number on public ledgers.

4. Moderation, Reporting & Verification Tiers

We enforce community safety through decentralized community reporting,Perspective API toxicity filtering, and transparent moderation:

• Citizen Talk: Community-monitored open discourse.

• Trust Circle & True Witness: Verified tiers with higher reputation standards and responsibilities for moderating flagged content.

Violations result in content flagging, reduced reach, temporary warnings, or permanent cryptographic account suspension.

5. Reporting & Good-Faith Appeals

If you encounter harmful content, use the in-app Report button on any post. If your post was flagged or moderated by mistake, you can submit an appeal through your profile dashboard for review by community stewards.

Our Shared Goal:

A healthier public square where truth can be spoken safely, evidence can be preserved immutably, and every good-faith citizen feels protected against malicious actors.
