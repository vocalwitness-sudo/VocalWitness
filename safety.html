Safety & Your Rights

Your Right to Speak the Truth

Every person has the right to share what they have witnessed. This right is protected under Article 19 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

Protected by: Google Cloud / Firebase Cloudflare Edge Shield Strong Cryptography

1. Strong Protection by Design

We cannot give away information that we never collected. Your identity stays protected.

  • No identity logs linked to your public posts.
  • Location and device details are removed in your browser before upload.
  • Strong technical protection for anonymous reporters.

2. Extra Safety Tips for High-Risk Situations

📡

Safer Internet Connection

In restrictive areas, use Tor or a trusted VPN. Avoid publishing on networks linked to your official ID.

📱

Device Safety & Quick Exit

Use a strong passcode. If you feel in danger, press the red Quick Exit button — it clears local data and leaves the site.

3. What Is Not Allowed

These actions are strictly forbidden:

  • ✕ Doxxing: Sharing someone’s private details without permission.
  • ✕ Harassment & Threats.
  • ✕ Fake or Altered Evidence meant to harm others.
  • ✕ Spam & Fake Accounts.

4. How We Keep the Community Safe

Safety comes from community reporting and clear verification levels:

• Open Talk — Open discussion for everyone.

• Higher Trust — Stronger protection and reputation.

Our Goal:

A safe public square where real reports can be shared and people are protected by design.

⚙️ Technical & Operational Details

Cryptographic & Subpoena Defense

VocalWitness uses client-side SHA-256 hashing and zero-knowledge proofs (zk-SNARKs). • Zero identity storage: IP addresses are not logged alongside public posts.
• Automatic EXIF scrubbing: Camera identifiers and GPS are stripped in the browser before upload.
• Mathematical protection: No central private keys exist that can unmask anonymous witnesses.

High-Risk Operational Advice

Prefer Tor or trusted VPNs in restrictive environments. Avoid mobile networks tied to government-issued SIMs. Use a strong device passcode (not only biometrics). The Quick Exit button immediately clears localStorage/sessionStorage and redirects to a neutral site.

Moderation Tiers

• Open Talk / Citizen Talk — Open discourse with community reporting.
• Higher Trust / True Witness — Cryptographically notarized evidence with reputation scores.
