1. Privacy & Data Protection Design

We apply data minimization principles to reduce unnecessary tracking and protect contributor privacy:

  • Focused Collection: Only necessary post and account data required for platform operation are processed.
  • Automated Sanitization: Metadata and device signatures can be stripped in-browser before publishing.
  • Secure Storage: Information is stored securely via Firebase and Cloudflare with operational controls.

See the full Privacy Policy for details on what is collected and how it is used.

2. High-Risk Operational Protocols

📡

Network Obfuscation

In restricted or sensitive environments, consider using secure connection tools or trusted VPNs to protect your browsing context.

📱

Device Security & Quick Exit

Maintain strong device lock safeguards. Press Quick Exit to instantly clear local browser storage and redirect away from the site.

3. Prohibited Conduct

  • ✕ Doxxing — Publishing non-public personal details without consent.
  • ✕ Harassment & Coercion — Targeted intimidation or threats.
  • ✕ Synthetic Disinformation — Fabricated or heavily manipulated media meant to deceive.
  • ✕ Spam & Sybil Attacks — Automated or fake accounts designed to abuse the system.

4. Community Governance & Trust Tiers

• Citizen Talk — Open public discussions moderated via community flags and platform oversight.

• Citizen Circle — Phone verification to reduce spam and bots.

• Witness Voice / Higher Trust — Reports backed by client-side media hashing and stronger integrity tooling.

Trust tiers and reputation are never for sale.
