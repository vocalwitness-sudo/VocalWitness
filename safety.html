Security architecture backed by: Google Cloud / Firebase Cloudflare Edge Shield Zero-Knowledge Cryptography

1. Mathematical & Subpoena Defense

We cannot surrender what we do not possess. VocalWitness uses client-side SHA-256 hashing and zero-knowledge proofs (zk-SNARKs).

• Zero Identity Storage: We do not log IP addresses alongside public testimonies.
• Automatic EXIF Scrubbing: Camera identities and GPS coordinates are stripped in your browser prior to ledger submission.
• Mathematical Immunity: Even when presented with state orders or subpoenas, zero-knowledge architecture ensures no central administrator holds private keys to unmask anonymous witnesses.

2. Operational Protocols for High-Risk Regions

📡

Network & Connection Security

Always connect through Tor or trusted encrypted VPNs when publishing from restrictive regimes. Avoid using personal mobile networks tied directly to government-issued SIM cards.

📱

Device Safety & Quick Exit

Keep your device protected with a passcode rather than biometric unlock. In physical danger, use the Quick Exit button at the top of the page to immediately flush local caches and leave the application.

  • ✕ Evidence Tampering & AI Fabrications: Uploading fabricated deepfakes or altered media designed to frame innocent parties.
  • ✕ Spam & Bot Sybil Networks: Deploying automated scripts or fake accounts to manipulate community moderation.
