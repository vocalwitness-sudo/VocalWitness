How the Square is Governed
DAO → ← Square

Co-owners of decisions — not buyers of truth

VocalWitness is a public square for sealed reports. Day-to-day speech uses the Feed (like a normal social post). Hard rules and rare shared decisions use the DAO (quadratic votes). Money never buys voting power or a higher “truth rank.”

1. Three zones

Zone What you do Money? Governance?
Feed Post, read, like / support signals, corroborate, report abuse Optional tips only Not DAO
DAO Propose & vote on policy, appeals, features, process; rare treasury Votes cost reputation budget, not cash Yes
Operations Keep servers up, legal minimums, security patches Platform costs Founder / stewards with transparency

2. What belongs on the DAO table (co-ownership)

Category Examples Bar
Policy What counts as testimony; privacy defaults; safety rules Normal quorum
Moderation appeal Challenge a remove/purge on a specific post Normal quorum + evidence
Feature Product changes the community wants prioritized Normal quorum
Process (governance) Who may propose; quorum; voting seasons Higher care
Treasury How to use shared support funds (servers, legal, critical tools) Highest bar — rare, public, detailed

You co-own how the Square is run, not a corporate share certificate. Quadratic voting: stronger preference costs more (strength²), so one loud actor is expensive to dominate.

3. What stays out of the DAO

ActionWhy
Likes / support on a normal post Engagement, not law-making
Corroboration (“I saw this too”) Evidence signal
Report spam or abuse Safety pipeline (mods + rules)
Personal tips to an author Private support — must not buy votes or rank
Optional “Support the Square” or press credential Keeps lights on — never sells trust tier or vote weight

4. Money — highest bar, minimal surface

Hostile actors (including state-linked accounts posing as ordinary citizens) often target money and ranking. So VocalWitness keeps the money surface small:

  • Personal tips / donations do not change vote weight or evidence rank.
  • Shared funds (if any) move only through an explicit Treasury proposal with clear amount, purpose, and public outcome.
  • Treasury proposals should be rare — running costs are mostly ops; governance is mostly rules and appeals.
  • Paying for an optional professional credential never buys a higher truth score.

Reality: many people prefer paying for a clear product over pure donations. Optional paid tools are allowed for sustainability; they are not a path into government of the Square.

5. Defenses against bad-faith “citizens”

  • Phone / tier checks reduce mass fake accounts (not perfect).
  • Quadratic cost makes flooding strong votes expensive.
  • Proposing is limited to higher-trust roles so the table is not pure spam.
  • Vote tallies are server-checked; clients cannot invent totals.
  • Sealed reports stay integrity-first; governance does not “edit truth” by popularity alone.

6. Safety layer (not the DAO)

Flagging synthetic media, steward consensus on removals, and testimony challenges are handled by the safety engine — separate from quadratic platform votes.
