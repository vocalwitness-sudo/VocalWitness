3. Architecture & Verification Tiers

To prevent automated spam and coordinated network manipulation, VocalWitness employs a layered trust architecture:

  • Witness Voice / True Witness: Advanced notarization tier utilizing client-side SHA-256 media hashing, metadata stripping, and Zero-Knowledge proofs ($\text{zk-SNARKs}$) to establish immutable proof-of-authenticity.
