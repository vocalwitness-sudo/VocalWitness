3. Verification Levels (How Trust Works)

To keep the square safe from bots and spam, we use simple levels of verification:

  • Citizen Talk — Open to everyone who signs up. Basic public discussion.
  • Trust Circle — Unlocked with a temporary phone check (used only to stop spam). Your phone number is never saved on the public ledger.
  • Witness Voice / True Witness — Highest level. Your media gets a digital fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) on your own device, private details are removed, and Zero-Knowledge proofs help prove the evidence is real without revealing your identity.
