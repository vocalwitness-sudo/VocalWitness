🛡️ Zero-Knowledge Subpoena Defense

We cannot surrender what we do not possess. VocalWitness is architected so that personal identifiable information (PII) never reaches our servers. In the event of court orders, state subpoenas, or legal demands from any jurisdiction worldwide, our response is mathematically fixed: Zero user identity logs exist to be disclosed.

Enforced via: Client-Side zk-SNARKs Local Canvas EXIF Scrubbing Zero IP Logging

1. Client-Side Data & Cryptographic Handling

VocalWitness combines client-side cryptography, automated metadata stripping, and zero-knowledge verification so you can speak truth without exposing unwanted personal details. When you attach evidence, processing happens entirely inside your browser:

  • Local EXIF Scrubbing: Camera identifiers, hardware details, and GPS coordinates are stripped in browser memory prior to upload.
  • SHA-256 Hashing: Your browser calculates a unique fingerprint locally. Only this digest and sanitized media are broadcasted.
  • Zero-Knowledge Proofs: Verification rules use client-side zero-knowledge proofs (zk-SNARKs) to confirm standing without revealing private credentials.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect or Store

To ensure absolute whistleblower safety, VocalWitness maintains a strict zero-retention policy on:

  • ✕ IP Addresses: Network requests routed through Cloudflare Edge Shield purge origin IP metadata; IPs are never indexed against public records.
  • ✕ Phone Numbers on Public Records: Phone verification exists solely as an ephemeral anti-spam gate. We never store, publish, or link phone numbers to public posts.
  • ✕ Government IDs, passports, biometric scans, or personal contact social graphs.
