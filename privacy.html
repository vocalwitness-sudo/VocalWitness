Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Your privacy is protected by strong technology — not just promises.

🛡️ We Cannot Give What We Do Not Have

VocalWitness is built so that your personal identity never reaches our servers. If any authority asks for your private information, our answer is simple: We have nothing to give.

Protected by: Client-Side Protection Automatic Metadata Removal No IP Logging

1. Everything Important Happens on Your Device

When you upload a photo, voice note, or document, the sensitive work is done inside your own browser before anything leaves your device:

  • Metadata is removed: Camera model, GPS location, and other private details are automatically stripped.
  • Digital fingerprint is created: Your browser makes a unique fingerprint of the file. Only this and the cleaned media are sent.
  • Strong verification: You can prove you are a real person without revealing private identity details.

2. What We Never Collect or Store

For maximum safety we deliberately do not keep:

  • ✕ Your real IP address — requests go through protection layers so your location is not linked to your posts.
  • ✕ Phone numbers on any public record — phone checks are only temporary to stop spam and are never saved or shown.
  • ✕ Government IDs, passports, biometrics, or your personal contact list.

3. What We Do Process (Only What’s Needed)

Account Basics

Your chosen username, security keys, and any trust badges you earn.

Published Content

Your reports, cleaned media, digital fingerprints, and timestamps.

Anti-Spam Checks

Proof that you are a real person — without revealing who you are.

Security Protection

Anonymous technical data used only to block attacks and bots.

4. Aligned with Universal Human Rights

Our design follows Articles 12 and 19 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights: everyone has the right to privacy and the freedom to share information without interference.

5. We Are a Neutral Platform

VocalWitness is only a technical host. We do not write, approve, or fact-check the content people post.

Each person is fully responsible for what they choose to publish.

6. What Is Not Allowed & How to Report

Harassment, sharing someone’s private personal details without consent, child sexual abuse material, and deliberately fake harmful media are strictly forbidden.

If you see content that breaks these rules, please report it using the in-app button.

7. Quick Exit & Permanent Records

You can instantly clear your local browser data with the Quick Exit button. However, once a report is published and locked, it becomes a permanent record.

8. Intellectual Property

The VocalWitness platform, including its software, design, name, and logo, is the exclusive intellectual property of VocalWitness. You may not copy or reuse it without permission.

⚙️ Technical & Legal Details

Client-Side Processing

EXIF metadata, GPS coordinates and device identifiers are stripped in-browser using Canvas / Web Crypto APIs before any upload occurs. Only the cleaned media + SHA-256 digest leave the device.

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Verification uses client-side zk-SNARKs so standing can be proven without revealing underlying credentials or identity. No private keys or identity logs exist on VocalWitness servers.

What is never stored

  • Origin IP addresses (purged at Cloudflare Edge)
  • Phone numbers on any public or permanent ledger
  • Government IDs, biometrics, or contact graphs

Intellectual Property

The VocalWitness platform (software, cryptographic systems, hashing methods, Zero-Knowledge implementation, Evidence Ledger design, interface, branding, name and logo) is the exclusive intellectual property of VocalWitness and is protected by copyright and applicable laws.
