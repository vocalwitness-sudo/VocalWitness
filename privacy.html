Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

At VocalWitness, we believe truth should never require sacrificing your safety. We build privacy-first technology designed to protect citizens while maintaining an undeniable public record.

Secured by industry standards: Google Cloud / Firebase Cloudflare Edge Shield W3C Web Crypto & ZK Proofs

1. Our Privacy & Trust Philosophy

VocalWitness combines client-side cryptography, automated metadata stripping, and zero-knowledge verification so you can speak truth without exposing unwanted personal details. We do not sell user data, run targeted advertisements, or track you across the web.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect or Store

  • Your Phone Number on Public Records: Phone verification exists solely as a temporary anti-spam check. We never store, publish, or link phone numbers to your public posts.
  • Government IDs, passports, or biometric scans.
  • EXIF camera metadata (GPS coordinates & camera hardware IDs are scrubbed automatically before media uploads).
  • Personal contacts, social graphs, or tracking cookies.

3. Information We Process

Account Identity

Chosen username, cryptographic keys derived on your local device, and optional profile badges.

Published Evidence

Testimonies, media attachments, tamper-evident SHA-256 hashes, and timestamp certificates.

Anti-Spam Verification

Zero-Knowledge proof receipts verifying human status and account trust without revealing underlying private details.

Security Operations

Anonymized security statistics routed through Cloudflare to block DDoS attacks and malicious bots.

4. Global Infrastructure & Third-Party Protections

We rely on world-class technical infrastructure to keep VocalWitness resilient and encrypted:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase: Secure enterprise database storage, encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Cloudflare DNS & WAF: Global edge caching and threat filtering to ensure high availability during public events.

5. Mathematical Safeguards (Zero-Knowledge Cryptography)

Where enabled, Zero-Knowledge proofs ($\text{zk-SNARKs}$) allow our protocol to confirm that a user meets platform credibility or human verification rules mathematically—without ever storing, seeing, or revealing their underlying private information.

6. Data Control & The Public Ledger

You may delete your account profile at any time. However, content published to the public testimony record functions as a permanent historical record. While metadata is stripped automatically, public statements cannot be altered retroactively once notarized by the ledger.

🛡️ Your Safety & Voluntary Disclosure

Your identity is under your control: Stating your name or speaking on record is entirely voluntary. If you choose to speak publicly, VocalWitness secures your statement with cryptographic timestamps.

Pro Tip for Citizen Safety: To protect yourself from harassment or targeted abuse, avoid sharing personal phone numbers, home addresses, or private financial details inside the body of your public testimony.
