Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about sealing, privacy, and verification.

What is VocalWitness?

A privacy-first public square where anyone can publish sealed citizen reports. Once sealed, ordinary users (including the author) cannot change the media, hashes, or core record. Text can only be corrected for 30 minutes. After that only soft-hide is allowed.

What does “sealed” mean?

Media is cleaned and hashed on your device with SHA-256. The final uploaded bytes receive a digital fingerprint. Strict server rules protect the seal so ordinary users cannot break it. Sealed means the bits are protected. It does not mean the event is proven true.

Can I edit or delete my report later?

Text is editable for 30 minutes only. After that you can soft-hide the report (it disappears from normal feeds but stays in the sealed record). Hard delete of sealed evidence is not available to authors. This keeps the record verifiable.

Does the hash prove that the event really happened?

No. Hashes and Evidence Packs only prove that the digital file has not been changed after it was sealed. They do not prove that the real-world event occurred. Integrity ≠ truth.

Is this a blockchain?

No. VocalWitness uses application-level sealing (client hashing + strict rules) plus optional daily public timestamping of the ledger root via OpenTimestamps. It is not a public blockchain.

What is the difference between Public Square and Witness Voice?

Public Square — open citizen reporting. Anyone can post.
Witness Voice — higher-integrity channel for first-hand observations. Media and text are hashed on-device with stronger sealing expectations.

What is Phone Verification / Citizen Circle / Witness Circle?

Phone verification raises your trust level (Citizen Circle) and helps reduce spam. Witness Circle (Higher Trust) is earned through platform checks and optional zero-knowledge membership proof. These tiers are never for sale.

Is my phone number made public?

No. Phone numbers are used only for anti-spam and trust scoring. They are not written onto the public sealed ledger as your identity.

How do journalists or NGOs verify evidence?

Use the Verify Evidence page or download the Evidence Pack. Full instructions are in the Journalist Guide.
