# Frequently Asked Questions ### What is VocalWitness? VocalWitness is a privacy-first public square where anyone can publish sealed citizen reports. Once a report is sealed, ordinary users (including the author) cannot change the media, hashes, or core record. Text can only be corrected for 30 minutes. After that only soft-hide is allowed. --- ### What does “sealed” mean? When you publish: - Media is cleaned (EXIF removed from photos, audio normalized) and hashed on your device. - The final uploaded bytes receive a SHA-256 fingerprint. - The report is protected by strict server rules so the seal cannot be broken by ordinary users. Sealed means the bits are protected. It does **not** mean the event is proven true. --- ### Can I edit or delete my report later? - Text: editable for 30 minutes only. - After 30 minutes: you can soft-hide the report (it disappears from normal feeds but remains in the sealed record). - Hard delete of sealed evidence is not available to authors. This keeps the record verifiable. --- ### Does the hash prove that the event really happened? No. Hashes and Evidence Packs only prove that the digital file has not been changed after it was sealed. They do not prove that the real-world event occurred. Integrity ≠ truth. --- ### Is this a blockchain? No. VocalWitness uses **application-level sealing** (client hashing + strict Firestore and Storage rules) plus optional daily public timestamping of the ledger root (OpenTimestamps). It is not a public blockchain. --- ### Why do you scrub EXIF and normalize audio? - Photos: EXIF (location, device info, etc.) is removed by default to protect privacy. - Audio: normalized so the published version is consistent and the hash matches the exact bytes that were uploaded. - Video: structural validation only — the original bytes are kept so the hash remains exact. --- ### What is the difference between Public Square and Witness Voice? - **Public Square**: Open citizen reporting. Anyone can post. - **Witness Voice**: Higher-integrity channel for first-hand observations. Media and text are hashed on-device with stronger sealing expectations. --- ### What is Phone Verification / Citizen Circle / Witness Circle? - **Phone verification** raises your trust level and unlocks stronger features (Citizen Circle). - **Witness Circle** (Higher Trust) is earned through platform checks and optional zero-knowledge membership proof. It is not for sale. - These tiers affect visibility and privileges. They do not change the sealing rules of individual reports. --- ### Is my phone number made public? No. Phone numbers are used only for anti-spam and trust scoring. They are not written onto the public sealed ledger as your identity. --- ### Can moderators delete reports? Moderators can soft-hide or take limited actions for safety and legal reasons. Sealed evidence is designed so that ordinary hard deletion by users or low-level accounts is blocked. Serious cases follow the platform’s safety and moderation process. --- ### How do journalists or NGOs verify evidence? 1. Open the shared link or the Verify page. 2. Download the Evidence Pack (JSON). 3. Re-download the media and check the SHA-256 hashes. 4. Follow the instructions inside the Evidence Pack. A detailed guide is available on the Journalist Verification page. --- ### What happens if I post false or harmful content? False, misleading, or harmful reports can result in content being hidden, account restrictions, or permanent bans. The sealed record remains for accountability where appropriate. --- ### Is the platform free? How is it funded? Basic use is free. Optional support from citizens helps keep the Square independent. Higher trust levels are earned, not purchased. --- ### Which languages are supported? The interface supports multiple languages including English, and several others (Hindi, Chinese, Amharic, German, Indonesian, and more). You can change language in your profile or settings. --- ### Still have questions? Contact us through the Feedback link or check the [How to Use](/how-to-use) guide for step-by-step instructions.
